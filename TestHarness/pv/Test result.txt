FINDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Reacquisition too slow in: cloud_step_down, cloud_step_up, cloud_ramp (worst 1.24 s against a 0.50 s limit).
    P&O moves duty by 0.002 every 10 ms, so the stage can slew 0.20 duty per second
    and no faster. This is a rate limit, not a tuning error -- raising dD or
    shortening the perturbation period are the only levers.

2.  DC bus outside 700 +/- 5% in: partial_600, low_light_250, cloud_step_down, cloud_ramp.
    The boost output feeds a fixed 98 ohm resistor, so bus voltage is whatever the
    duty and the available power make it. Nothing regulates it. The integrated
    system holds this bus at 700 V with the grid-side loop -- see
    config/harnessParams.m. Until this stage is tested against a stiff bus rather
    than a resistor, its MPPT behaviour here does not predict its behaviour there.

3.  Steady-state power ripple over 2% in: low_light_250.
    P&O dithers around the peak by construction; the size of the dither is set by
    dD = 0.002. Reducing it lowers ripple and slows reacquisition -- the two specs
    pull in opposite directions and dD is the single knob between them.
